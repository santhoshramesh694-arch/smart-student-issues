Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers to common questions about tracking issues, assigning staff, and submitting satisfaction reviews.

Any registered student with a valid campus email and roll number can report grievances. Faculty and administrative personnel can also file issues directly through the portal.

Upon submitting an issue, you receive a unique tracking code (e.g. ISS-2026-0001). In your Student Dashboard, you can click on any issue to view a real-time visual timeline showing assignment, inspection in progress, and resolution notes with photo evidence.

The assigned staff member submits resolution details and an optional photo showing the completed repair. The student receives an in-app notification and is prompted to submit a 1 to 5-star rating with optional comments.

Campus Problem Analytics is an administrative intelligence engine that clusters issues by physical location (Blocks, Labs, Hostels) and category. It alerts campus management to structural patterns—such as recurring plumbing leaks in Block A or Wi-Fi degradation in Block B—enabling proactive preventive maintenance.

Every status transition is immutably stored in the issue_updates table with the user ID, timestamp, and remark. In addition, the central audit_logs table tracks every administrative change, assignment, and deletion.
