How Smart Campus Operates

A fully transparent 4-stage issue tracking process connecting students, technicians, and campus leadership.

1. File Grievance

Student snaps a photo, sets priority and location, and receives an instant tracking code.

2. Smart Assignment

Administrators route tasks to electrical, IT, or plumbing technicians based on active workloads.

3. Physical Fix & Proof

Staff inspects, repairs the breakdown, and uploads resolution notes and completion photos.

4. Rate & Analytics

Students grade service quality while campus analytics flags recurring infrastructure hotspots.
